Beyond
the title.

Marketing should be an investment that compounds, not an expense that drains. Monastic Marketing was built on that conviction.

I founded the agency in 2018 out of frustration with vanity metrics, opaque reporting, and firms more interested in protecting a retainer than moving a client’s revenue. What started as a reaction has become a full-stack growth partner: media buying, technical SEO, and CRM automation under one roof.

For scaling companies, that means transparent reporting, measurable financial impact, and systems that keep producing after a campaign ends. Two US hubs — Utah and Texas — serving clients across North America and beyond.

The 60 second executive briefing

01 / Who I am

An operator’s perspective.

I’m Tyler Barton, founder of Monastic Marketing. I help ambitious businesses become market leaders through data-driven marketing and automation that generates predictable, compounding revenue.

02 / What I’ve built

From frustration to a full-stack partner.

I founded Monastic Marketing in 2018, frustrated by vanity metrics, opaque reporting, and agencies more focused on retainers than client ROI. It now operates as a full-stack growth partner across media buying, technical SEO, and CRM automation, with GSD CRM as the system underneath.

03 / The scale

Experience across clients and campaigns.

340+ clients scaled, $24M+ in revenue generated, and 1,200+ campaigns launched, held to a 5/5 client satisfaction rating. Two US hubs, in Utah and Texas, serve clients across North America and globally.

04 / How I lead

Transparency. Speed. Discipline.

Six principles govern the work: radical transparency, speed and execution, results obsession, true partnership, continuous innovation, and monastic discipline. Ora. Labora. Convertere. Pray, work, convert.

05 / Where I create value

Connecting strategy to the work.

The work connects paid media, technical and local search, creative, CRM implementation, and automation. The focus is moving companies past campaign-by-campaign marketing toward connected systems, clear attribution, and revenue that compounds.
