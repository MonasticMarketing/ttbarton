The executive profile Perspective shaped by practice

Beyond
the title.

I’ve owned a company, led the teams inside it, and built the revenue and technology systems other companies run on.

I work as a fractional CMO, CTO and CRO — taking ownership of a function a company needs run properly but is not ready to hire for at full cost. Demand and brand as CMO. Systems, data and the technology stack as CTO. Pipeline, forecasting and revenue as CRO. The distinction from consulting is accountability: I am measured on the number, not the recommendation.

That position is earned rather than claimed. I was owner and COO of Soulegria, a therapeutic treatment and coaching organization, through a seven-figure exit — so I have carried payroll, service delivery, and the consequences of a bad quarter. I founded Monastic Marketing in 2018 and built it to 85+ employees and contractors, 340+ companies scaled, and $24M+ in client revenue generated, with the CRM and automation layer those teams run on built in-house.

Owner-led companies rarely need another vendor. They need someone who has held the seat before to build the operating system underneath the business — reporting leadership actually trusts, pipeline that does not depend on one person, a stack that talks to itself — and then to make the team capable of running it without me. Based in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas, working with companies across North America.

The 60 second executive briefing

01 / Who I am

An operator’s perspective.

I’m Tyler Barton. I work as a fractional CMO, CTO and CRO for owner-led companies — taking ownership of marketing, revenue or technology rather than advising from outside it. Owner and COO through a seven-figure exit; founder of Monastic Marketing.

02 / What I’ve built

From frustration to a full-stack partner.

I founded Monastic Marketing in 2018, frustrated by vanity metrics, opaque reporting, and agencies more focused on retainers than client ROI. It now operates as a full-stack growth partner across media buying, technical SEO, and CRM automation, with GSD CRM as the system underneath. I was also owner and COO of Soulegria, a therapeutic treatment and coaching organization, through a seven-figure exit.

03 / The scale

Experience across clients and campaigns.

A seven-figure exit, 340+ clients scaled, $24M+ in revenue generated, 1,200+ campaigns launched, 2,000+ people trained, and a team of 85+ employees and contractors. Based in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas, serving clients across North America and globally.

04 / How I lead

Transparency. Speed. Discipline.

Six principles govern the work: radical transparency, speed and execution, results obsession, true partnership, continuous innovation, and monastic discipline. Ora. Labora. Convertere. Pray, work, convert.

05 / Where I create value

Connecting strategy to the work.

The work connects paid media, technical and local search, creative, CRM implementation, and automation. The focus is moving companies past campaign-by-campaign marketing toward connected systems, clear attribution, and revenue that compounds.
